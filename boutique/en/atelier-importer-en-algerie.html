Workshop — importing into Algeria: procedure and real cost

From domiciliation to clearance, and the six items that blow up your landed cost.

← Back to the shop

Two subjects, because in practice they are inseparable: the procedure, where an error of sequence blocks an entire shipment; and the landed cost, where six financial items that are rarely anticipated turn an expected margin into a loss.

The workshop cites the instruction and the note in force with their references, so participants can verify them for themselves.

What you receive

  • One day: the regulatory framework of domiciliation, the documentary chain, customs clearance.
  • The real landed cost computed on a worked case, item by item.
  • A landed cost workbook to take away.

Included

  • A written answer to questions raised, within 15 days

Not included

  • Handling an import operation in progress
  • Customs advice in the regulatory sense, which belongs to the customs broker
  • Any approach to a bank or the administration
