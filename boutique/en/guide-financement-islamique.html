Guide to Islamic finance for companies

Murabaha, ijara, musharaka: the formulas actually distributed in Algeria, and what they cost.

← Back to the shop

Islamic finance is distributed in Algeria by several institutions, under names that resemble one another and mechanisms that differ markedly. The guide compares them on the only ground that concerns a director: what the formula actually costs, and what it requires.

The guide does not address religious compliance. That assessment belongs to the competent bodies, not to a financial practice. We describe mechanisms, costs and procedures.

What you receive

  • A PDF guide: each formula, its mechanism, its real cost and its use case.
  • A comparison table against conventional credit, at equal amount and term.
  • The documents required, by type of formula.

Included

  • Updates for 12 months

Not included

  • Any opinion on religious compliance — we are not a sharia authority
  • Preparation of your file
  • The pricing terms of any particular bank, which are negotiated
