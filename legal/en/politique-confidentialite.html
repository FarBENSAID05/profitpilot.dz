For what purpose, and on what basis

The law requires that data be collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes, and not subsequently used in a way incompatible with them. Ours number four.

1
Answering your enquiry. You write, we reply. Basis: your own approach, and your consent to being contacted back.
2
Quoting for and performing an engagement, where the contact leads to a professional relationship. Basis: pre-contractual steps and then the contract itself.
3
Sending you our publications, if and only if you subscribed. Basis: your consent, withdrawable at any time and without justification.
4
Meeting our legal obligations — accounting, tax and professional — and responding to duly empowered authorities. Basis: legal obligation.

No other purpose is pursued. In particular, an enquiry about a service does not sign you up to the newsletter: those are two separate consents.

Your rights under Law 18-07

Law no. 18-07 of 10 June 2018, as amended and supplemented by Law no. 25-11 of 24 July 2025, grants you rights that we give effect to on a simple request, free of charge.

RightWhat it entitles you to obtain
Information
(art. 32)		To be informed, expressly and unambiguously, before any collection — which is what this page is for
Access
(art. 34)		Confirmation that processing concerns you, its purposes, the categories of data, the recipients, and communication of your data in intelligible form together with any available information on its origin
Rectification
(art. 35)		Updating, rectification, erasure or blocking of data whose processing does not comply with the law, in particular where it is incomplete or inaccurate
Objection
(art. 36)		To object, on legitimate grounds, to processing concerning you — and, without having to justify yourself, to the use of your data for marketing purposes

A deadline that binds us. Article 35 requires the controller to carry out the necessary rectifications, at no cost to the applicant, within ten days of being seised. If we refuse or stay silent within that period, you may refer the matter to the national data protection authority, which appoints one of its members to conduct the necessary investigations and have the rectifications made.

Article 37 further prohibits direct marketing by electronic mail, automated calling system or fax towards a person who has not given prior consent. We do not market that way; any message you receive that does not follow from your own subscription should be reported to us.

The right of rectification may be exercised by the heirs of the person concerned.

How to exercise these rights. Write to contact@profitpilot.dz stating the right invoked. Proof of identity will be requested: this is not red tape, it is what stops a third party obtaining your data under your name. You also retain the right to refer the matter directly to the national data protection authority.

Professional secrecy: a protection that adds to this one

Exchanges that turn into an engagement fall under a separate and more demanding regime than data protection. The professional secrecy binding a statutory auditor covers all information obtained in the course of the engagement.

In practice, that means information relating to a file is disclosed to no one — no more to a curious third party than to a commercial partner — outside the cases the law requires and the persons you expressly designate.

That regime sits on top of this policy: where the two diverge, the more protective one applies.
