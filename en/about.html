Our mission

Supporting Your Success

ProfitPilot NextGen Consulting was born from a simple conviction: every business, whatever its size, deserves access to professional-grade financial and economic analysis in order to make the best decisions.

Based in Algiers, BENSAID Farouk has since 2024 supported companies in sectors as diverse as equipment import, agrifood, public works and retail, with a personalised and rigorous approach.
